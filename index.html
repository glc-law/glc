Our story

A firm with a different starting point

We’ve grown. Our reason for being has stayed the same.

GLC Law began in a small 200-square-foot space on Thomas Road, with room for just four desks. Today, our team works from a purpose-designed office on Paul Street in London.

Almost 100 people work together in a custom-designed, state-of-the-art office with dedicated training rooms, breakout areas and an on-site amphitheatre. It has room for twice as many people, with space to collaborate, learn and grow.
